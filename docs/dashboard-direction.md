# Dashboard surface brief

Mode: Operate. Users need to identify current environmental conditions, data freshness, and state transitions quickly on desktop or phone. The supplied brief and explicit confirmation pin the visual direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a quiet classroom monitoring workspace, with temperature condition and truthful connection state ahead of historical investigation.

OWN-WORLD: cool neutral ground, white bordered surfaces, restrained green accent, Geist interface type, compact Lucide icons, and color-plus-text severity badges. Dark mode preserves these relationships.

STORY: sign in, verify freshness, scan the three implemented sensors, then investigate charts and transition logs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a narrow left navigation, slim classroom/account header, page title and connection label, condition strip, three compact sensor panels, then a wide chart beside device connectivity. On phones the same reading order becomes one column with a menu.

FORM: user-pinned minimalist SaaS dashboard. Native selects, semantic forms, bounded histories, and explicit offline/error states support operation over decoration. No separate concept seed applies to the pinned direction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
